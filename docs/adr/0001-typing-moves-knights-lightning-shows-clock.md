# Typing moves the Knights; the Lightning shows the clock

The Knights' position is not tied to the clock. They move only while Fencing, chasing the Lightning, which marks elapsed clock time, and they close the Gap faster the further behind they are (it halves every two minutes of Fencing). We rejected letting the Knights follow the clock, which would make the typing detection pure decoration. We also rejected letting typing alone move them as a productivity score, which would leave the widget unable to show the time remaining, and the user explicitly does not want a "did I type the whole time" measure. The Knights' position is stored as covered clock time, not as a place on the Panel, so changing the Finish time or the panel width rescales the Knights and the Lightning together and the Knights can never pass it.
